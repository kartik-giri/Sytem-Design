# The questions before Design the app I should ask.
- What should it do?
- How well much it do it?

1. Functional requirement:
- I should ask what features the app should have.
- For example singup,upload ohoto, scrool the feed.
- All theses are functional requirements. If requirement is missed app is incomplete.

2. Non-Functional requirement:
- It describes the quality of app.
- The feed loads does it 200 ms or 20 sec.
- These aren't features , they are quality like speed, duarability, scale, cost, availability etc.

# Turn vague requirements into measurable ones
- Interviewers often give vague statements such as "The app should be fast and scalable."

- Your job is to turn them into concrete targets.
Vague statement	Better requirement
The app should be fast	95% of live drawing updates arrive within 150 ms under the target workload
The app should scale ->	Support 10,000 concurrent connections across multiple gateway instances
